# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers filling remote roles in AI and IT. They arrive from a resume, a LinkedIn profile or an application, usually on a laptop, and give the page a short look before deciding whether to dig in.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Anthony Jaimez, an IT support technician with more than five years on enterprise help desks who builds AI agents for IT operations. Success: after the first screen, the visitor opens the live demo of Agent Ops Lab, the featured project.

## Positioning

A help desk technician who builds AI that troubleshoots the way he does: evidence first, a person approves every change. Every project is live and can be tried, and the featured one runs on a real server he breaks on purpose.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare this page against other candidates' portfolios and resumes. They follow links out to live demos, GitHub source, LinkedIn and a PDF resume.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, CSS Modules, deployed on Vercel from GitHub (gkitad/portfolio).
- All copy lives in `src/content.ts` and stays as written: same facts, projects, experience and wording. Design and order may change.
- Public copy never uses the em dash or en dash.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy must sound human and precise, never AI-written.
- The result must not look AI-made or templated.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real screenshots of each project from live runs: `src/assets/work/*.jpg`.
- Live demos and source links for four projects, test results stated in `content.ts`.
- Resume PDF at `public/Anthony_Jaimez_Resume.pdf`.
- No photo of Anthony, no testimonials, no employer logos. None of these may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work running before describing it.
2. Every claim on the page is something he did and can show.
3. Safety and human approval are part of the story, not fine print.
4. Plain words over buzzwords.
